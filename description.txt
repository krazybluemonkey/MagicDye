The spiritual successor to the Dye Hard mod!

- Features -
- Adds 32 Magic Dye "slots" that are customized through the Magic Dye Vat.
- Adds the Magic Dye Vat, a pre-boss dye vat upgrade that has a 33% chance not to consume crafted dye ingredients. It's the heart of the mod.
- A Dye research system that reuses journey mode research! needed for Magic Dye customization options.
- Compatible with most modded dyes. (to a degree, see homepage for details)
- Custom "Color Modifier" system to add even more customization options. Modders can add their own. (see homepage for details)
